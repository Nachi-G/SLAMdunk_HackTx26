Inside the planner

Inflated A* finds a spatial route. Cubic segments use p(u) = p₀ + (3u² − 2u³)Δp, with duration bounded by velocity, acceleration and jerk. Swept checks preview moving obstacles 0.7 s ahead. Synthetic patrols yield when the vehicle enters their clearance boundary.

SANDO math approximation in 2D at flight altitude. Stop-to-stop segments have acceleration discontinuities at joins. This browser does not run the ROS/Gurobi optimizer.
