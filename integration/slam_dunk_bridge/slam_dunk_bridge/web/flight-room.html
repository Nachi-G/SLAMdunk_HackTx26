How the drone plans

SANDO-inspired obstacle inflation, A* routing and cubic trajectories with speed, acceleration and jerk bounds inside each segment. Planning is horizontal at the chosen altitude; obstacles use conservative full-height collision bounds.

This runs a browser approximation, not the full ROS/Gurobi SANDO solver. Segment joins can have acceleration discontinuities. Moving objects yield at the drone’s boundary.
